# Reading list

Acquire only when the phase starts. One book at a time, finished.

## Phase 0 — feedback loop

- **Natural Number Game** — browser, free, no install. Build ℕ from the Peano
  axioms; end by proving commutativity of addition. Search "Lean 4 Natural
  Number Game"; it has changed hosts more than once.
- **Mathematics in Lean** — <https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathematics_in_lean>
- **Lean Zulip** — <https://leanprover.zulipchat.com> (the closest thing to office hours)
- **Mathlib docs** — <https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/>

## Phase 1 — proof

- **Hammack, _Book of Proof_** — free PDF: <https://www.people.vcu.edu/~rhammack/BookOfProof/>
- Velleman, *How to Prove It* — fallback only if Hammack doesn't land.
- Richmond & Richmond, *A Discrete Transition to Advanced Mathematics*, 2nd ed.
  (AMS, 2023) — **problem bank, not a second textbook.** AMS member free eBook,
  2026. Local only: `reading/AMSTEXT_63.E.pdf`, gitignored (copyrighted). Use:
  extra problems when a Hammack chapter runs thin (Ch. 1–2, 5–6 overlap
  Hammack); Ch. 11 continued fractions pairs with Silverman in Phase 2.

## Phase 2 — number theory

- **Silverman, _A Friendly Introduction to Number Theory_**

## Phase 3 — abstract algebra

- **Pinter, _A Book of Abstract Algebra_** (Dover, ~$16)

## Phase 4 — branch (pick exactly one)

- Axler, *Linear Algebra Done Right*, 4th ed. — open access:
  <https://linear.axler.net/>
- Abbott, *Understanding Analysis*
- Spivak, *Calculus* — overlaps Abbott; pick one
- Graham, Knuth & Patashnik, *Concrete Mathematics*

## North stars (years out)

- Arnold, *Mathematical Methods of Classical Mechanics*
- Type theory / Curry–Howard

## Browsing, any time

- Aigner & Ziegler, *Proofs from THE BOOK*
- Richard Borcherds' YouTube lectures
- 3Blue1Brown — the trailer, not the film
